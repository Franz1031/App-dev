The Maze Runner: The Death Cure (2018)

> *“Every maze has an end.”*

---

Overview

**The Maze Runner: The Death Cure** is a 2018 American dystopian science-fiction film directed by **Wes Ball** and based on the novel *The Death Cure* by **James Dashner**.  
It’s the final installment in *The Maze Runner* trilogy, following *The Maze Runner* (2014) and *The Scorch Trials* (2015).

Thomas leads his group of escaped Gladers on their final mission: to break into the legendary **Last City**, a labyrinth controlled by **WCKD**, to rescue their friend **Minho** and uncover the truth about the **Flare virus**.

Plot Summary

After escaping from WCKD, **Thomas** and the remaining Gladers embark on a dangerous rescue mission to save **Minho**, who has been captured and is being experimented on for a cure to the **Flare virus**.  
Their journey leads them to the **Last City**, WCKD’s heavily fortified base. Inside, Thomas confronts **Teresa**, who betrayed them but still seeks to find a cure.  

The Gladers face devastating losses — including **Newt’s death** — as Thomas and his friends fight to free the captives and end WCKD’s reign.  
In the end, the surviving Gladers find a peaceful haven, safe from the Flare, symbolizing hope and renewal for humanity.

Themes

- **Friendship & Loyalty:** The bond between the Gladers drives their survival.  
- **Sacrifice:** The cost of saving others is central to the story.  
- **Hope vs. Control:** WCKD’s obsession with control contrasts Thomas’s faith in human will.  
- **Science & Morality:** Explores the ethics of using humans as test subjects.

Trivia

- Filming was delayed after **Dylan O’Brien** suffered a severe on-set injury in 2016.  
- It’s the longest and most action-packed movie in the trilogy.  
- Major differences exist between the film and the book’s ending.  
- Filmed using IMAX cameras for enhanced clarity in action sequences.
